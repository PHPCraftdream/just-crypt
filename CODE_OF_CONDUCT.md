# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors are expected to be respectful, constructive, and clear. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, and unwanted sexual attention are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository maintainer or [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/PHPCraftdream/just-crypt/security/advisories/new). Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation when needed to keep the project safe and useful.
